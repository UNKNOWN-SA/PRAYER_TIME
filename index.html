⚖️ Asr Prayer Calculation (Madhab)
📌 Shafi'i / Maliki / Hanbali: Asr begins when the shadow of an object equals its length (1x). Generally earlier.
📌 Hanafi: Asr begins when the shadow equals twice the object's length (2x). Generally 30–60 min later.
